FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS — UniHub
=================================
Last updated: 2026-10-27

The requirements below are derived from the finalized project scope.
Each is grouped by the feature area it belongs to.


FR-01  CAMPUS NAVIGATION
-------------------------
FR-01.1  The system shall display department locations with name, type,
         floor, latitude, longitude, and directions.
FR-01.2  The system shall list faculty members with their department,
         office location, name, and designation.


FR-02  GUIDELINES AND RULES
-----------------------------
FR-02.1  The system shall display guidelines filtered by category and
         department.
FR-02.2  Each guideline shall have a title, body, and last-updated date.


FR-03  DEADLINES AND ANNOUNCEMENTS
------------------------------------
FR-03.1  The system shall display upcoming deadlines with title, due
         date, category, and department.
FR-03.2  The system shall display announcements with title, author,
         department, and published-at timestamp.


FR-04  COURSES
--------------
FR-04.1  The system shall list courses filtered by department and
         semester.
FR-04.2  Each course shall have a code, title, department, and semester.


FR-05  SOCIETIES AND EVENTS
-----------------------------
FR-05.1  The system shall list societies with name, how-to-join
         information, and contact details.
FR-05.2  The system shall display events linked to a society and
         location, with title and start time.


FR-06  FAQs
------------
FR-06.1  The system shall display FAQs filtered by category.
FR-06.2  FAQs shall be browsable without logging in.
FR-06.3  Each FAQ shall have a question, answer, category, and
         last-updated date.


FR-07  COMMUNITY Q&A
---------------------
FR-07.1  A logged-in student shall be able to post a question.
FR-07.2  A logged-in student shall be able to post an answer to a
         question.
FR-07.3  Answers shall have an is_approved flag; only approved answers
         are shown as trusted.
FR-07.4  Visitors (not logged in) shall be able to view approved Q&A.


FR-08  PERSONAL CHECKLIST
--------------------------
FR-08.1  The system shall maintain a list of checklist items (with type,
         title, and sort order).
FR-08.2  Each student shall have a personal checklist tracking which
         items are done.


FR-09  USER MANAGEMENT
-----------------------
FR-09.1  Each user shall belong to a department and have a name, email,
         role, and batch year.
FR-09.2  The system shall support three roles: Visitor, Student, and
         Admin/Faculty.
