PROJECT OVERVIEW — UniHub
=========================
Last updated: 2026-10-27

PURPOSE
-------
Every year a new batch of freshers joins the university. In the beginning
they don't know any seniors and are mostly surrounded by other freshers
who are just as lost. They don't know where departments and offices are,
which rules and guidelines apply to them, what deadlines are coming, or
whom to ask.

UniHub exists to be that guide. It is a single platform that replaces
the senior a fresher doesn't have yet.


SCOPE
-----
UniHub covers the following areas:
- Department locations, offices and faculty (with floor and directions)
- Guidelines and rules (department-specific)
- Upcoming deadlines and announcements
- Courses by department and semester
- Societies, how to join them, and their events
- FAQs (accessible without login)
- Community Q&A with moderated answers
- Personal checklist for new-student tasks


DESIGN PRINCIPLES
-----------------
1. Clarity first
   A confused fresher should find an answer within a few clicks.

2. Trustworthy information
   Official content comes from admins/faculty. Community answers are
   approved before they count as reliable.

3. Organized around departments
   Most content is tied to a department, since that is how a fresher
   thinks about the university.

4. Simple and readable
   This is a student software engineering project.
